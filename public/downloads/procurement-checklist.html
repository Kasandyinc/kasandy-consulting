Canadian Procurement
Readiness Checklist

Before a buyer reads a single line of your proposal, they check 24 things. This checklist covers all of them — organized by the order buyers actually review them.

How to use this: Work through each section before your next bid submission. Any unchecked item is a potential disqualifier. Aim for 22 or more before submitting.
01 Business Registration & Legal Standing
Business is legally registered in Canada (federal or provincial)
Sole proprietorship, corporation, partnership, co-operative, or non-profit
Business Number (BN) from CRA — 9-digit number starting with your GST/HST account
Register at cra-arc.gc.ca or call 1-800-959-5525
GST/HST registration — required for contracts over $30,000/year
Applies even if your current revenue is below the threshold
Provincial business licence current and valid
Requirements vary by province and business type
WSIB / WCB compliance certificate (current)
Required for most federal and Ontario provincial contracts
Articles of incorporation or business registration doc on file and accessible
02 Insurance & Financial Requirements
Commercial general liability insurance — minimum $2M per occurrence
Most federal tenders require $2M; some corporate buyers require $5M
Professional liability / E&O insurance if providing professional services
Insurance certificate names the buyer as additional insured (if required by the RFP)
CRA tax compliance — no outstanding tax debts or payment plans in default
Government buyers verify CRA status; a tax debt can disqualify a bid
Banking & financial capacity to deliver the contract scope
Some large tenders require financial statements or credit references
Bid bond / performance bond if required by the specific tender
Required for some construction, IT, and large service contracts
03 Supplier Certifications & Registrations
Registered on buyandsell.gc.ca (Supplier Registration Information)
Federal government's primary supplier database — free to register
NAICS codes correctly assigned to your actual service/product categories
Registered on provincial portals relevant to your target markets
BC Bid, Biddingo (Ontario), MERX (multi-province), etc.
Diversity certification obtained or in progress (CAMSC, CCAB, WBE, BEBC Society)
Required for some supplier diversity programs; preferred in many others
Industry-specific certifications current
Health Canada, CFIA, ISO, professional designations — varies by sector
Security clearance if required
Federal contracts often require Reliability Status or Secret clearance — plan 3–6 months ahead

04 Capability Statement & Documentation
Capability statement is current — updated in the last 6 months
Core competencies listed by procurement category (not general descriptions)
Past performance documented — 3–5 references with scope, outcome, client type
NAICS/commodity codes visible in capability statement
Differentiators are specific — evidence-based, not marketing language
Contact information is current and includes a primary contact name
05 Before You Submit — Final Check
Read mandatory requirements section first — every item checked
Response follows evaluation matrix structure and weighting
Pricing is competitive (research comparable contracts before pricing)
Submission format correct — file type, naming, page limits, font size
Submission deadline confirmed — date, time, time zone
Debriefing requested after any unsuccessful bid
